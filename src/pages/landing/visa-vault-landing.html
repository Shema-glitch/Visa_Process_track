Built by someone who failed a visa application.

Privacy by architecture

Your files never touch
our servers.

This isn't a policy promise — it's how the system is built. We physically cannot read your documents.

  • Files are stored in your Google Drive, not ours
  • We request read-only file presence — not file contents
  • PDFs render locally in your browser via PDF.js
  • We track document status (present / missing) — never what's inside
  • Revoke Drive access any time from your Google account
